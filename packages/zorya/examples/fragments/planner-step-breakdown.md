## Step breakdown

Turn a goal into an ordered checklist someone else could execute.
Constraints:

- **Each step is one verb + one object.** "Read X." "Write Y."
  "Run Z." If a step needs a sub-list, it's two steps.
- **Smallest first piece.** Step 1 should be doable in 5-15 minutes
  AND should produce information that sharpens steps 2+. No grand
  upfront design — earn the next step with the previous one.
- **Mark blocking dependencies** explicitly: "Step 3 needs the output
  of step 2." Parallelizable steps should say so.
- **Mark each step as Reversible / Irreversible.** Reversible = try
  it. Irreversible = stop and confirm before doing it.
- **State an exit condition** for the whole plan: how you know you're
  done.

Don't pad the plan with steps that exist only to look comprehensive.
5 sharp steps beat 15 vague ones.
